Before You Book.

The questions people ask most — answered up front.

I'm Ready to Book
Is the check-up really free?

Yes. There's no charge for the Financial Check-Up and no obligation to buy anything.

Will you try to sell me something?

If I see a gap, I'll tell you and explain your options. You decide if and when to act — no pressure, no follow-up calls you didn't ask for.

I already have life insurance through work. Do I still need this?

Maybe not — that's what the check-up is for. Work coverage often ends when the job does and is usually a small multiple of salary. We'll look at whether it's enough.

I don't have a lot of money. Is this for me?

Yes. Protection matters most when money is tight. We start with where you are today.

I've heard IUL and annuities are scams. Are they?

They're legitimate tools that are sometimes sold to the wrong people. They're right for some situations and wrong for others — I'll tell you plainly if one doesn't fit you.
